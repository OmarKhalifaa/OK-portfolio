Overview

One shared foundation for a growing product ecosystem.

Vodafone’s digital products were evolving quickly across platforms, markets, and product teams. The work needed a common design language that could make delivery faster without flattening the character of each experience.

The Master Design System Kit brought foundations, tokens, components, documentation, and contribution workflows into one dependable product for designers and engineers.

01 · The problem

Consistency was being rebuilt project by project.

01

Fragmented foundations

Teams used similar patterns with different spacing, colour, and interaction decisions.

02

Repeated effort

Designers and engineers recreated familiar product patterns instead of reusing them.

03

Uneven quality

Accessibility and responsive behaviour varied depending on the project and delivery pressure.

02 · Solution

A system designed as a product, not a static library.

The system connected semantic tokens to reusable components, then wrapped both in clear usage guidance. This made decisions visible and gave teams a predictable path from design to production.

Contribution and release workflows made the library sustainable: product teams could propose improvements while the core squad protected quality and coherence.
